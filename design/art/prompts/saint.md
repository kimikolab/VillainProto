# 聖女（2 章で勇者に加わる・1 章の締めに影で出る）

題材 2（指示書 §5-2）。`gen --job saint --n 4 --size 1024x1536 --transparent`。採用したら `design/art/story/聖女.png`（前の期の口 `saint_shadow.png` も読む）。
ライゼル（白い鎧・金の縁・紺の下衣・金の太陽の紋）と並べて同じ陣営に見えること。顔は見せない。

Composition: one character from the waist up, vertical 1024x1536 canvas, figure centered with safe margins,

Subject: a holy maiden of the kingdom's church who travels with the hero - a young adult woman of slender build

navy-blue inner collar and sash, and a small gold eight-pointed sun emblem at the chest. Both hands are clasped
gently in front of her chest around a simple gold-and-white prayer charm; fingers correct and relaxed. Posture
upright, quiet, composed, slightly ominous in its serenity. Palette: warm white, pale gold, a little navy -
matching a white-and-gold armored knight. No halo, no glowing magic, no wings, no staff crossing the body.
