# 伝令（魔王軍本隊の使い）

題材 4（指示書 §5-4）。`gen --job courier --n 3 --size 1024x1536 --transparent`。採用したら `design/art/story/伝令.png`。
味方の陣営（魔王軍）の側の人物。顔は影。

```text
Composition: one character from the waist up, vertical 1024x1536 canvas, figure centered with safe margins,
the top of the hood fully inside the frame. Three-quarter view, body turned toward SCREEN LEFT.
Genuinely transparent background (RGBA), nothing behind the figure.
Subject: a messenger sent by the main host of a demon lord's army - a tall, thin figure of indeterminate age
wrapped in a heavy black travelling cloak with a deep hood. The hood's shadow hides ONLY the eyes and brow: a soft
dark shadow falls across the upper face so the eyes cannot be seen, but the lower half of the face is clearly
drawn and lit - a narrow nose, thin expressionless closed lips, a pale angular jaw and chin. The inside of the
hood is dark grey cloth, not a flat black void. The cloak is plain, road-worn black wool
with a dull charcoal lining, fastened at the throat by a single dark iron clasp shaped like a closed eye. One
gloved hand emerges from the cloak holding out a sealed dispatch: a rolled parchment tied with a dark red cord
and a blank dark-red wax seal (no symbol, no writing). The other hand stays hidden. Impersonal, bureaucratic,
unreadable posture - an official who delivers orders and does not explain them. Palette: black, charcoal, a
single small dark-red accent. No weapons, no horns, no glowing eyes, no magic effects.
```
