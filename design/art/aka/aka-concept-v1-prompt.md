## 生成プロンプト

Use case: stylized-concept.
Create a polished character concept art sheet for a Japanese dark-fantasy tactical RPG, a new playable ally named 血詠みのアカ (Aka, Blood-Reader).
Core story: an excommunicated practitioner of forbidden magic who calmly exploits the blood spilled by allies' self-damaging powers, accumulates it, and releases powerful area magic. Flavor for characterization only, do not print: 「禁術に手を出して破門された。代償を払うのが自分である必要は、どこにも書いていなかった。」
Design proposal: androgynous adult human mage, slender, poised, intelligent eyes and a small knowing self-satisfied smile, emotionally composed. Dark wine-red medium-length hair, pale natural skin. A recognizable elegant silhouette, layered former clerical scholar robes in charcoal and muted ivory with restrained deep crimson lining, a visibly removed insignia on the stole suggesting expulsion. Practical closed clothing and boots, no sexualization. Strong readable shapes and restrained detail, around six-head stylized adult proportions.
Blood-reading magic: scattered small dark crimson droplets float inward from outside the figure, transmuting into fine crimson handwritten magical script above one open hand and coiling into concentric suspended written rings around a dense red core. The other hand precisely conducts the writing. This is a calculating spellcaster harvesting an existing cost, not someone cutting their own body. No wounds, knives, gore, corpses, blood drinking, fangs, bat wings or horns. Blood looks like dark red liquid transitioning into luminous calligraphic magic, not flame or smoke.
Composition: landscape concept sheet on light warm gray paper-like backdrop. One dominant complete full-body three-quarter standing design on the left with full head and boots visible and enough clear margin; on the right a close-up of the same face plus two smaller coherent casting studies of that SAME character: gathering small droplets into written rings, then opening the rings into a forceful broad outward magical discharge. Clean spacing, hierarchy, no boxes, no duplicate unrelated designs, no scenery, no other characters. Keep effects controlled so costume and hands remain legible.
Style: high-quality Japanese RPG illustration, precise expressive dark linework, controlled cel shadows and soft painted material transitions, appealing game-character readability rather than gritty photorealism. Restrained saturated crimson focal accents. No text, labels, logo or watermark. Character design exploration, not production sprite.
