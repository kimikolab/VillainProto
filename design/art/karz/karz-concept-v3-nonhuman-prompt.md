## デザインの意図

人の顔・髪・衣服・体格を外し、呪いが凝り固まった異形を提案。
銅の六角印を核に、割れた黒い殻・不規則な眼・根のような枝肢・黒煙で形を構成。
枝肢の先端で呪印を刻む。人型案とは独立した検討案であり、採用は未確定。

## 生成プロンプト

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: alternative creature character concept for Karz (祟りのカーズ), an original Japanese dark-fantasy RPG curse spirit.
Primary request: a completely NONHUMAN CURSE ENTITY, a separate design direction from a humanoid ghost. Curses themselves have coagulated into an eerie sentient creature. Invent a memorable readable monster silhouette with no human face, hair, clothes or human anatomical structure.
Style/medium: refined Japanese 2D anime fantasy monster concept art, fine crisp ink contours, restrained layered cel shading, detailed but controlled material texture, muted charcoal and old copper palette. Same illustrated game aesthetic as beautiful weathered anime RPG characters, adapted to an inhuman creature. Not photoreal, not 3D.
Composition: portrait 2:3 full-creature concept, one isolated creature centered against a plain warm pale-gray studio backdrop, full form and every tendril inside frame with generous margins, faint ground shadow. Strong identifiable silhouette that remains legible at game portrait scale. No layout panels or text.
Creature design: a floating asymmetrical CURSE HUSK, about the overall height of a person but entirely alien in construction. Its principal mass is a vertically twisted hollow crescent of blackened, cracked, bark-like ceramic shell, curling around a large suspended copper HEXAGONAL SEAL that is its living core. The crescent has a broad broken upper hook, a compact gnarled central root-knot, and a tapered lower coil. The core is inset in a dark open cavity; a small number of thin nested copper hexagonal grooves glow dimly within it. The shape should feel like a malignant ancient curse that grew its own shell, with beautiful negative spaces. Irregular curved interlocking shell plates, matte charcoal exteriors, dusty ash-gray chipped edges and rusty old copper exposed inside. No humanoid torso or pelvis, no shoulder line, no separate human-shaped head.
Face substitute: the upper hooked husk has just THREE small uneven amber incisions like barely opened eyes embedded at unnatural angles in the shell, no whites, no pupils, no mouth, nose, ears or skull. It suggests hostile attention without becoming a human face. A few fine hexagonal cracks link those incisions to the core.
Appendages: THREE long rootlike jointed curse-limbs grow at different heights and different directions from the central knot, clearly not a pair of human arms. One reaches forward and ends in two long calligraphic hooked prongs like an engraving nib; one curls up behind the shell; one curves downward. Thick readable branch structure tapering into ink-dark pointed tips, not lots of thin spaghetti. The forward tip inscribes a SINGLE small dim copper hexagonal mark near its point, communicating that this entity brands enemies with deepening curses. Its lower body is a twisting thick ink-smoke root which splits into two short ragged vapor trails hovering above the ground, without feet or legs.
Supernatural details: black ink vapor seeps quietly from the hollow core and open cracks. Two or three small broken shell fragments hang near the crescent, suggesting unstable cohesion. Keep the material forms solidly drawn and the smoke sparse and subordinate. The entity looks ancient, patient and malicious rather than energetic or cute. The hexagonal mark is a curse brand, not an electronic screen.
Lighting: soft neutral lighting so all shapes are readable; very restrained ember-copper light inside the core and tiny eye incisions. Dominantly charcoal-black, ash gray, weathered rust and tarnished copper. No bright neon.
Constraints: original standalone NONHUMAN spirit design. One entity, one core, one small curse mark. No human face, human skin, human hair, human hands, clothing, robe, cloak, mummy wrappings, skeleton, horns, wings, human body proportions, conventional grim reaper, generic hooded ghost, character from an existing franchise, large background magic circle, landscape, scenery, decorative frame, words, labels, watermark, gore or blood. Not a heap of miscellaneous clutter, not a round cute blob. Strong deliberate nonhuman crescent-and-root silhouette.
