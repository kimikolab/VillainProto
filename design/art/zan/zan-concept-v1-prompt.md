3. Eyes emotionally strained and obsessively fixated, NOT an impassive cool assassin.
Zan: human-looking adult man, lean sinewy compact build, natural human ears and skin, species deliberately ambiguous between human and human-looking demon. No horns, tail, wings, unusual skin color or glowing eyes. Dark nearly black slightly uneven medium-short hair, stray bangs framing but NOT obscuring eyes. Restrained grief, sleepless fixation and barely-contained urgency; not blank, smug, seductive, relaxed or screaming.

Full-body standing concept, NOT crouched, running, attacking or doing acrobatics. Face three-quarter toward viewer, both intense eyes visible.

No other characters, no scenery, no corpses, no blood spray, no magic or particles, no face mask covering expression, no text, no labels, no watermark. Polished original game-character concept matching supplied rendering style, but a completely distinct character.
